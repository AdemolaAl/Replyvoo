# Service Availability Policy

This policy explains how available we aim to keep Replyvoo, how we handle maintenance and outages, and what happens if the Service is unavailable because of us. It forms part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).

## 1. Our target

We aim to keep Replyvoo available **99.5% of the time each calendar month**, measured as the ability of your AI Closers to receive and answer messages on connected channels and of you to log in to the dashboard. The free Spark plan is provided as available, without the service credits in section 5.

## 2. Planned maintenance

- Most updates happen without downtime. Some updates (for example database changes) need a short pause, usually a few seconds to a few minutes.
- When we expect a pause longer than 10 minutes, we tell you in the app or by email at least 24 hours before, and we plan it for quiet hours where we can.
- During maintenance the website and dashboard may show a "back soon" page. Messages that arrive during a short pause are usually delivered to Replyvoo again by the messaging platform when we are back.

Planned maintenance announced as above does not count as downtime.

## 3. What depends on others

Replyvoo connects to services we do not control, including Telegram and other messaging platforms, AI providers, payment processors, email providers and our hosting provider. When one of them has a problem, part of Replyvoo may not work. We reduce the impact where we can, for example:

- if our main AI provider is down, Closers can switch to a backup AI provider;
- if a reply fails because of an error, the conversation charge is returned automatically;
- payment confirmations are checked again when you come back from the payment page and by our team, so a delayed confirmation does not lose your order.

Outages of third-party services, of your own bot or account (for example a revoked token or a platform restriction), or caused by your use against our terms, do not count as downtime under this policy.

## 4. During an incident

- We work to restore the Service as fast as possible, at any hour.
- We post updates in the app and, for longer incidents, email workspace owners.
- After a serious incident we explain what happened and what we are changing.

## 5. Service credits

If, in a calendar month, Replyvoo was available less than 99.5% of the time because of a fault on our side, workspace owners on a paid plan can ask for a service credit:

| Monthly availability | Credit |
| --- | --- |
| Below 99.5% | 3 extra days on your current plan |
| Below 99.0% | 7 extra days on your current plan |
| Below 95.0% | 15 extra days on your current plan, or a refund of 50% of that month's plan fee if you prefer |

Ask within 30 days after the end of the month by emailing support@replyvoo.com with your workspace name and the times you were affected. Credits are added to your plan period and have no cash value except where the table says so. Service credits are your only remedy for downtime under this policy, without affecting refunds required by law or under our [Billing, Credits & Refund Policy](/legal/refunds).

## 6. Your data

We back up the database regularly and keep backups for 14 days, so we can restore it if something goes wrong. You can export your workspace data at any time from Settings.
