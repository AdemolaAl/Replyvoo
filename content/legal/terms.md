## 1. What Replyvoo does

Replyvoo lets businesses create AI Closers that automatically reply to their customers' messages. Today the Service works with Telegram, including Telegram bots, Telegram groups and personal Telegram accounts connected through Telegram Business. WhatsApp, Instagram and TikTok channels are marked "coming soon" and may launch later, change, or not launch at all.

The Service also includes a shared inbox, lead scoring, broadcasts ("Blasts"), payment links, a wallet of prepaid "Voo Credits", subscription plans, coupons, the Voo Squad referral programme, and Juno, an AI assistant that helps you set up and run your workspace. ### Connected Zedapex apps (Joinvoo)

Joinvoo is a separate app, also operated by Zedapex Limited, that brings customers from your ads to Telegram and tracks their joins, sign-ups and deposits. In Joinvoo you can pick Replyvoo to answer the chats of a Telegram bot, or of a manager's Telegram account connected to Joinvoo through Telegram Business. You link the two apps with the same Voo account. Joinvoo stays the only app connected to that bot or account: it keeps the bot token and the connection, and it sends every reply your AI Closer writes, so your customers only ever see your own bot or your manager's own account.

- **What is shared, and why.** When you link the apps and switch a bot or account to Replyvoo, Joinvoo shares with Replyvoo, and Replyvoo shares back with Joinvoo, only to provide this feature: the content of the messages in those chats (text, photos, voice notes, videos, files, stickers and button presses), the customer's Telegram profile data (name, username, language and the Telegram ID used to route replies), the ad source Joinvoo matched (platform, campaign, ad set, ad and country), and their sign-up and deposit information (number, value and date). Neither app uses this data for any other purpose, and Replyvoo never receives your bot token.
- **Separate subscriptions.** Each app has its own plans, prices, terms and refund policy, and each is billed separately. Chats that come through Joinvoo are normal Replyvoo conversations on your Replyvoo plan and credits, including the free allowance on Spark (currently 30 chats a month). Paying for Joinvoo does not give you a Replyvoo plan, and the other way round.
- **If Replyvoo can't answer.** If Replyvoo is unavailable, has an error, or your workspace has no conversations or credits left, Joinvoo's Team inbox takes the chat automatically. You remain responsible for every message sent from your bots and your managers' accounts, including messages your AI Closer writes.
- **Disconnecting.** You can disconnect one bot or account, or Joinvoo altogether, in More → Settings → Connected apps, or switch it back to Team inbox in Joinvoo, at any time. From then on no new messages are shared. Chat history already shared stays in each app under that app's own retention rules, and is deleted when you delete it there or close that account; you can also ask us to delete it sooner.
- **Who is responsible for the data.** Zedapex Limited operates both apps and is the controller of your account data in both. For the data of the people who write to your bots and managers, we act for you in both apps, as our [Data Processing Addendum](/legal/dpa) explains.
