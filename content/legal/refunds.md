# Billing, Credits & Refund Policy

This policy explains how you pay for Replyvoo, how Voo Credits work, and when refunds are available. It forms part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms). Where this policy says something is non-refundable, that is always subject to any rights you have under laws that cannot be excluded.

## 1. Plans

Replyvoo offers the following plans:

- **Spark**: free, with limited features and conversations.
- **Starter**, **Growth**, **Pro**, **Business**, **Titan** and **Black**: paid plans with increasing numbers of included conversations, AI Closers, channels, team seats and features.

The current price, included conversations and limits for each plan are shown on our pricing page and at checkout. The details shown when you pay are the ones that apply to that period.

Paid plans are **paid in advance** for:

- **one month**; or
- **one year**, usually at a discount compared with paying monthly. On a yearly plan, your included conversations reset every 30 days.

## 2. What counts as a conversation

Your plan includes a number of conversations per billing period.

- A **conversation** is one chat with one customer within a 24-hour window, including up to 10 AI replies.
- If the same customer keeps chatting after 10 AI replies, or after the 24-hour window ends, a new conversation starts.
- Messages you or your team send manually from the inbox do not count as AI replies.
- Unused included conversations do not roll over to the next billing period, unless your plan says otherwise.

When you reach your included conversations, your AI Closers can keep working by using Voo Credits from your wallet. If you have no credits left, AI replies will pause until you add credits, upgrade, or your next period starts. Your inbox will still receive messages.

## 3. Voo Credits

Voo Credits are prepaid units held in your Replyvoo wallet. You can use them for:

- extra conversations beyond your plan;
- power-ups and add-ons, such as extra features, higher limits or premium AI options;
- other items we price in credits from time to time.

The credit cost of each item is shown in the app before you use it.

**Important rules about credits:**

- **Prepaid.** You must buy credits before you use them.
- **No expiry.** Credits do not expire while your account is open.
- **Non-transferable.** Credits belong to the workspace that bought them. They cannot be sold, transferred, or moved to another account or workspace, except where we choose to allow a transfer between workspaces you own.
- **No cash value.** Credits are not money, a deposit, a stored-value product or e-money. They cannot be exchanged for cash, except where the law requires.
- **Non-refundable.** Credits are non-refundable once bought, except where the law requires or as described in section 8 below.
- **Promotional and referral credits.** Credits given free, through coupons, or as Voo Squad rewards have no cash value and are never refundable. Voo Squad rewards for a payment that is later refunded or charged back may be removed.
- **Account closure.** If your account is closed because you broke our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) or [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use), any remaining credits are forfeited.

## 4. Payments

Prices are set in US dollars. At checkout you see the payment methods that work in your country and the exact amount you will pay:

- **Paystack** and **Flutterwave**: cards, bank transfer, USSD and mobile money. Where you pay in a local currency, the amount is the US-dollar price converted at the rate shown at checkout.
- **Bachs**: cards, bank transfer, mobile money and USDT/USDC, for customers in the African countries Bachs serves. Where naira pricing applies, you pay in naira at the rate shown at checkout; elsewhere you pay in US dollars.
- **Gatevoo**: USDT on TRON (TRC20) or Bitcoin. The coin amount is locked when the payment page opens. Your order activates by itself once the payment is confirmed on the blockchain.
- **Manual USDT transfer** (only when offered): send exactly the amount shown to the address shown, and include your order reference. Our team activates the order after confirming the transfer.

Every payment is also subject to the processor's own terms. We do not see or store your full card details. An order is only activated after the processor confirms the payment to us directly; a "paid" screen alone is not enough.

- Any processing cost of a payment method is already built into the price shown for that method. There is never a separate fee line, so the same plan can cost slightly more or less with different methods. Your own bank or wallet may add its own charges, which are outside our control.
- Prices may not include taxes such as VAT. Where we must charge tax, it is shown at checkout.
- **Crypto payments** must use the exact network, amount and address shown. Payments sent to the wrong address or network, or short-paid, may be lost or stay unactivated until our team sorts them out, and cannot always be recovered. Confirmed crypto payments are final, except for refunds under section 8.
- Unpaid checkouts expire. Card and bank checkouts that are not completed within 48 hours are closed; crypto payment pages expire after 1 hour. Start a new checkout if that happens.

## 5. Renewal and auto-renewal

**Card plans (Bachs).** When you pay for a plan with a card through Bachs, the plan renews automatically:

- **On by default.** Auto-renew is switched on for card plans, and the checkout tells you this before you pay. At the end of each month or year we charge the same card the plan price then in force, until you cancel.
- **Reminder before every charge.** We email you at least 3 days before every renewal charge, with the plan, the amount and the date.
- **One-click cancel.** You can turn off auto-renew at any time with one click on the Plan page. No email or call is needed. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you already paid for; then the workspace moves to Spark and no further charge is made.
- **Failed payments.** If a renewal charge fails (for example the card has expired or has no funds), we email you and try the charge again. Your plan keeps working during the grace period stated in that email. You can update your card or pay another way on the Plan page at any time. If the charge still fails at the end of the grace period, auto-renew turns off and the workspace moves to Spark. Your AI Closers, chats, leads and unused credits stay.

**Other payment methods.** Plans paid by bank transfer, USSD, mobile money or crypto are prepaid and are never charged automatically.

- We email you **3 days before** your paid period ends.
- To renew, pay again from the Plan page. Paying before the end of the period adds the new period to the end of the current one, so you lose nothing by renewing early.
- If you do not renew, your workspace moves to the free **Spark** plan when the period ends. Your AI Closers, chats, leads and unused credits stay; features and limits above Spark stop until you buy a plan again.

**Auto Top-up** is a separate automatic charge. If you switch it on, we buy your chosen credit pack with your saved card when your credits run low (at most once every 6 hours). You can switch it off at any time in your Voo Wallet.

**Price changes.** We email you at least 14 days before a new price applies to your next renewal, so you can cancel first. A period you already paid for is never repriced. If you pay in a local currency such as naira, the US-dollar price is what we notify you about; the local amount of each renewal follows the exchange rate at the time, and the reminder email before each charge shows the exact amount.

## 6. Changing plans and stopping

**Buying a different plan** (higher or lower) starts it immediately. The unused value of your current paid period is not lost: it is converted into extra days on the new plan at the new plan's daily price. Your included conversations start again with the new plan.

**Buying the same plan again** extends your current period, as described in section 5.

**Stopping.** Cancel auto-renew with one click on the Plan page, or, on a prepaid plan, simply do not renew. You keep your paid features until the end of the period you paid for. Stopping does not delete your data or your credits. To delete your account, see our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

## 7. Coupons and promotions

- Coupons and promo codes apply only as described when issued, for example to a specific plan, billing period or first payment.
- Unless stated otherwise, a coupon can be used once per customer, cannot be combined with other offers, and has no cash value.
- Coupons may have an expiry date.
- We may cancel coupons, and reverse discounts or credits, obtained by fraud, abuse, multiple accounts or in breach of our terms.
- Discounts usually apply only to the period stated. After that, the normal price applies.

## 8. Refunds

**Plan payments.** You may ask for a full refund of a plan payment (new or renewal, monthly or yearly) within **7 days** of paying, provided you have not used a substantial part of the plan's included conversations or features in that time. After 7 days, plan payments are non-refundable, except where the law requires.

**Voo Credits** are non-refundable once bought, except where the law requires or in the cases below.

**Power-ups** follow the same 7-day rule as plans, unless the power-up is a service we have already started (for example Done-for-you setup after the work has begun).

**Auto-renewal charges.** If you forgot to cancel auto-renew, ask within 7 days of the renewal charge like any plan payment. If a renewal was charged after you cancelled, or after a failed charge we had already given up on, we refund it in full.

**We will refund, regardless of the above, when:**

- you were charged twice or the wrong amount by mistake;
- you paid for an order that was never activated and we cannot activate it;
- the Service was materially unavailable for a long period because of our fault (we may give a partial refund or credits instead, as set out in our [Service Availability Policy](/legal/availability));
- we close your account without cause, in which case we refund the unused part of prepaid plan fees;
- consumer protection law that applies to you requires it.

**We will not refund when:**

- the 7-day window has passed;
- you did not use the Service or your credits;
- your account was suspended or closed for breaking our terms;
- a third-party platform such as Telegram restricted or banned your account or bot;
- you were unhappy with AI output that matched your own settings and training;
- the credits were promotional, referral or coupon credits.

**What a refund does.** When we refund a payment, what it bought ends at once: a refunded plan ends and the workspace moves to Spark; credits from a refunded pack are removed from your Voo Wallet (never below zero); a refunded power-up is switched off. A partial refund may leave the purchase active if we agree so in writing.

**Purchases paid with Joinvoo credits.** If you paid for a plan or a Voo Credits pack from inside Joinvoo with your Joinvoo credits, the same rules apply. A refund goes back to your Joinvoo balance as Joinvoo credits (100 Joinvoo credits = US$1), not to a card or bank account. If Replyvoo did not confirm such a purchase, Joinvoo returns the credits automatically.

## 9. How to request a refund

Email support@replyvoo.com from the email address on your account and include:

- your workspace name;
- the order reference (it starts with RV-, and is in your payment receipt email and in Voo Wallet → Orders);
- the reason for your request.

We aim to reply within 5 business days. Approved refunds go back through the processor you paid with, to the original payment method where possible; it can take 5 to 15 business days to reach you. Refunds of naira payments are made in naira, for the naira amount you paid. Crypto refunds are made in the coin you paid with (USDT or Bitcoin) to a wallet address you confirm by email, for the coin amount you paid, less network fees.

## 10. Chargebacks and payment disputes

Please contact us before disputing a payment with your bank: most problems are fixed faster that way.

If a payment is disputed or charged back:

- we may pause the workspace while the dispute is open;
- if the payment is reversed, what it bought is taken back as if it were refunded (section 8), and any Voo Squad reward paid to someone else for that payment is reversed;
- we may recover dispute fees charged to us if the dispute was unjustified, and we may close accounts that repeatedly file unjustified disputes;
- if the dispute is decided in your favour after you used the Service, we may ask you to pay again for what you used.

## 11. Invoices and records

We email a receipt for every payment, and every order is listed in Voo Wallet → Orders with its reference, amount, currency and status. If you need a formal invoice with your company name, address or tax number, email support@replyvoo.com with the order reference and those details.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes do not affect payments made before the change, except where required by law. The "updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed.
