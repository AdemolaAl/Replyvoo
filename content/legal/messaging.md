# Messaging and Anti-Spam Policy

This policy explains how you may contact people through Replyvoo: replies from your AI Closers, automatic follow-ups (Nudge) and broadcasts to your audience (Blasts). It forms part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and sits alongside our [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use). It is written for the businesses that use Replyvoo ("you").

Messaging apps protect their users hard. One spam campaign can get your bot, your account or your phone number banned, and can put the connection every Replyvoo customer relies on at risk. These rules protect you as much as anyone.

## 1. Who you may message

You may only send messages to people who:

- started the conversation with you, for example by messaging your Telegram bot, writing to your Telegram Business account or using the chat on your website; or
- clearly agreed to hear from you on that channel, and have not since asked you to stop.

Replyvoo is built this way on purpose. A Blast only goes to people who have already chatted with one of your connected Telegram bots in a private chat. It never goes to Telegram groups, to chats on your personal account through Telegram Business, or to people who blocked your bot. You must not try to get around these limits.

You must not:

- upload, buy, rent, scrape or import lists of phone numbers, usernames or chat IDs and message them;
- add people to groups or channels without their consent in order to message them;
- create extra bots or accounts to reach people who blocked you or to get around a platform's limits;
- message children, or anyone you know does not want to hear from you.

## 2. Blasts

A Blast is a one-off message to one of your audiences (everyone who chatted, hot leads, people who chatted but did not buy, quiet contacts, or customers who bought).

- **Relevant and expected.** Send only messages your audience would reasonably expect from you, related to what they asked about or bought.
- **Identify yourself.** Every Blast must make it clear which business it comes from.
- **Easy to stop.** Tell people how to stop receiving messages, for example "Reply STOP and we won't message you again", and honour it. When someone asks you to stop, block them in **Leads** (Replyvoo then skips them in every Blast and follow-up), and do not message them again from any account.
- **Reasonable frequency.** Do not send more than one promotional Blast to the same audience in a day, and keep the overall volume reasonable for your business. Replyvoo may slow down or pause sending that triggers platform limits or complaints.
- **Honest content.** No misleading subject lines or first lines, fake urgency, fake winners, or messages pretending to come from someone else.
- **Billing.** Blasts are paid for with Voo Credits. Credits for people a Blast could not reach (for example, because they blocked your bot) are returned to your Voo Wallet automatically.

## 3. AI Closer replies and Nudge follow-ups

- Your AI Closers reply when a customer writes to you. That is not spam, provided the replies are honest and on topic.
- **Nudge** sends at most one automatic follow-up to a warm or hot lead who went quiet, inside the conversation they started. Do not use manual messages or extra tools to keep chasing people who have not answered.
- If someone asks your AI Closer to stop, the conversation must end. Train your Closer to accept "stop", "unsubscribe" and similar words and to hand the chat to a human, and block the contact in Leads.

## 4. Platform rules come first

Every channel has its own rules, and they apply in addition to this policy:

- **Telegram**: the Telegram Terms of Service and the Bot Platform rules, including the ban on spam and on bots that message users who did not start them.
- **WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger and TikTok** (when they open on Replyvoo): each platform's business messaging and commerce policies, including WhatsApp's 24-hour customer service window and its rules on templates and opt-in.
- **Email and SMS** you send outside Replyvoo are your responsibility, but you must not copy contact details out of Replyvoo to spam people elsewhere.

If a platform restricts or bans your bot or account because of how you used it, that is between you and the platform, and it is not a reason for a refund.

## 5. Laws on electronic marketing

You are responsible for following the laws on electronic marketing and data protection that apply to you and to the people you message, which may include consent rules, the right to object to direct marketing, and rules on promotions, prizes and price claims. If you are not sure whether you may send a message, do not send it.

## 6. What we do about spam

We watch for signs of spam, such as sudden large Blasts, high block rates, platform warnings and complaints. We may, with or without notice:

- pause a Blast or a channel;
- limit Blasts for a workspace;
- suspend AI Closers, channels or the account;
- remove credits obtained through abuse; and
- close accounts for serious or repeated spam, as set out in our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).

## 7. Report spam

If you received a message from a business using Replyvoo that you did not ask for, tell us at support@replyvoo.com. Include the bot or account name, the date and a screenshot if you can. We review every report.
