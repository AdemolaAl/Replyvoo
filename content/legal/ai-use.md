# AI Use Policy

Replyvoo uses artificial intelligence to answer your customers ("AI Closers") and to answer your questions about your own sales ("Juno"). This policy explains how that AI works, what you can expect from it, and the rules for using it. It forms part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).

## 1. How AI Closers work

An AI Closer writes each reply using a large language model. It follows the role, personality, knowledge, rules and limits you give it in Agent Studio, and the conversation so far. It can:

- answer questions from your knowledge (Q&A, products and prices, policies, imported pages and files);
- qualify leads and mark them cold, warm, hot or won;
- share your payment link (PayLink) and read payment screenshots and other images;
- understand voice notes, when voice transcription is switched on;
- send one follow-up (Nudge) to a lead who went quiet;
- hand the chat to your team when your handoff rules say so.

**Pro Brain** uses a more capable model for the Closers you choose. If our main AI provider is unavailable, Replyvoo may switch to a backup AI provider so your Closers keep answering.

## 2. AI makes mistakes

AI output can be wrong, incomplete or out of date, even when your knowledge is correct. It can misread a question, a voice note or an image, and it can say something you did not intend.

- **Test before you go live.** Use the test chat and the scenario tests in Agent Studio, and fix any answer you don't like with **Fix this answer**.
- **Watch real chats.** Check Pulse Inbox and the Gaps list regularly, and keep training your Closer.
- **Keep a human in the loop** for refunds, complaints, large orders, legal, medical or money questions. Turn on the handoff rules that fit your business.
- **Payment screenshots are not proof of payment.** An AI Closer reading a receipt is a convenience, not a bank confirmation. Always check that the money arrived before you deliver.

You are responsible for what your AI Closers say to your customers, as if your staff had said it.

## 3. Be honest that customers are talking to AI

- If a customer asks whether they are talking to a person or a bot, your AI Closer must not claim to be human. Do not train it to deny being AI.
- Where the law where you or your customers are requires you to say that a chat is automated, you must say so, for example in your bot's description or first message.
- Do not give your AI Closer the name or photo of a real person without that person's permission, and never present it as a celebrity, public official, regulator or another company.

## 4. What AI Closers must not be used for

In addition to our [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use), you must not use AI Closers or Juno to:

- promise guaranteed profits, fixed returns, "risk-free" trades or bets, or guaranteed results of any kind;
- give individual financial, investment, trading, betting, legal or medical advice as if from a licensed professional;
- pressure, threaten or manipulate people, or target people who are vulnerable or under 18;
- collect passwords, one-time codes, card numbers or other secrets in a chat;
- make decisions that have legal or similarly serious effects on a person (for example credit, housing or employment decisions) without a human reviewing them;
- write content that is illegal, hateful, sexual involving minors, or that infringes someone's rights;
- impersonate a real person or business, or deceive people about who they are dealing with.

We may block, change or stop replies that break these rules, and pause the Closer or the account.

## 5. Your data and AI providers

- To write a reply, we send the AI provider the conversation, your Closer's instructions and the relevant parts of your knowledge. Voice notes are sent to a transcription provider when you switch transcription on.
- We use AI providers through their business APIs. Under their terms, data sent through those APIs is not used to train their public models.
- **We do not use your conversations or knowledge to train AI models**, ours or anyone else's. We use them only to run your Closers and Juno, to show you your own reports, and to keep the Service secure.
- Our current providers are listed in our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) and [Data Processing Addendum](/legal/dpa).

## 6. Juno

Juno answers your questions about your own workspace (chats, leads, sales and usage) and, on our website, answers questions about Replyvoo. Juno can be wrong too: check important numbers in your reports before you act on them.

## 7. Billing when the AI fails

You pay for conversations, not for errors. If an AI Closer cannot reply because of a technical error on our side or our AI provider's, the conversation charge for that reply is returned automatically.

## 8. Changes and feedback

AI models and providers change quickly. We may change the models we use when it improves quality, cost or reliability, and we will update this policy when what it says changes. Tell us about harmful or wrong AI output at support@replyvoo.com.
