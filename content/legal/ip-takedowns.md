# Intellectual Property and Takedown Policy

We respect intellectual property and expect everyone who uses Replyvoo to do the same. This policy explains how to report content that you believe infringes your rights, how the business concerned can respond, and what we do. It forms part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).

Replyvoo hosts content that our business customers add: their AI Closers' knowledge and instructions, website chat settings and logos, Blast messages, and the replies their AI Closers send. Each customer is responsible for having the rights to what they add.

## 1. What you can report

- **Copyright**: someone copied your text, images, product photos, course material or other original work into their AI Closer, website chat or messages.
- **Trademarks**: someone uses your brand name or logo in a way that suggests they are you or are endorsed by you.
- **Impersonation**: an AI Closer or bot presents itself as you, your business or one of your staff.

For spam, scams or other misuse, see our [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use) and [Messaging and Anti-Spam Policy](/legal/messaging), or email support@replyvoo.com.

## 2. How to send a takedown notice

Email **legal@replyvoo.com** with the subject "Takedown notice" and include:

1. your name, address, phone number and email;
2. the work or trademark you own (for a registered trademark, its registration number and country);
3. where the infringing material appears on Replyvoo: the bot username, website address where the chat appears, screenshots of the replies or messages, and dates;
4. why you believe the use is not authorised by you, your agent or the law;
5. a statement that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the owner of the right, or authorised to act for the owner; and
6. your physical or electronic signature.

Incomplete notices may take longer, because we may need to ask for the missing details.

## 3. What we do

- We confirm receipt within 3 business days.
- If the notice is complete and the claim is credible, we disable or remove the material, or ask the customer to remove it within a short deadline, and we tell the customer what was reported. We may share your notice, including your contact details, with the customer so they can respond, unless you ask us not to and the law allows.
- In urgent or clear cases (for example impersonation used to take money from people), we may pause the AI Closer, channel or account at once.

## 4. Counter-notice

If your content was removed and you believe that was a mistake, or that you have the right to use it, email **legal@replyvoo.com** with the subject "Counter-notice" and include:

1. your name, contact details and workspace name;
2. the material that was removed and where it appeared;
3. why you believe it was removed by mistake or that you have the right to use it; and
4. a statement that this information is accurate, and your signature.

We will send the counter-notice to the person who made the original notice. If they do not tell us within 10 business days that they have started legal proceedings, we may restore the material.

## 5. Repeat infringers

We close the accounts of customers who repeatedly infringe other people's rights, in appropriate circumstances.

## 6. False notices

Do not send notices or counter-notices you know are false. Abuse of this process may make you liable for the costs and harm it causes, and we may ignore further notices from you.

## 7. Our own rights

Replyvoo, Voo Credits, Voo Squad, Juno, AI Closer, our logos, software, designs and documentation belong to Zedapex or our licensors, as set out in our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms). If you think we used your material without permission, use the process above and we will look into it.
