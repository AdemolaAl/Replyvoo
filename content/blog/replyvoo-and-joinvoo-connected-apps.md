Joinvoo and Replyvoo are two apps made by Zedapex. They do different jobs, and they work best together.

- **Joinvoo** brings people from your ads to Telegram, and tracks who joins, signs up and deposits.
- **Replyvoo** is an AI salesperson. Your Closer answers their messages in seconds, day and night.

You sign in to both with the same Voo account.

## How it works

1. **Joinvoo brings the customer.** Someone taps your ad and starts your bot, or writes to your manager. Joinvoo matches them to the ad they came from.
2. **Replyvoo's AI talks to them.** Your Closer answers here in Replyvoo. Every reply goes out from your own bot, or from your manager's own Telegram account, so the customer only ever sees you.
3. **Your team can jump in anytime.** Take over in Pulse Inbox, or in Joinvoo's Team inbox. Hand the chat back to your Closer when you are done.

In your Pulse Inbox these chats are marked **via Joinvoo**: "via Joinvoo · @your_bot" for a bot, or "via Joinvoo · Sara's Telegram" for a manager's account. The top of each chat shows the ad the person came from and whether they signed up or deposited, so your Closer knows who it is talking to.

## How to turn it on

In Joinvoo, open **Channels**, tap a bot or a manager, then **Chats**. Under **Who answers chats?**, pick **Replyvoo**. That is the whole setup. If you don't have a Replyvoo account yet, Joinvoo opens Replyvoo already logged in with your Voo account; when you go back, the switch finishes by itself.

## Good to know

- **Joinvoo stays connected to your bot.** It keeps the token and sends every reply. Replyvoo never needs your bot token, and nothing else is connected to your bot.
- **History syncs both ways.** Your Closer sees what your team wrote in Joinvoo, and Joinvoo's Team inbox shows every reply sent from here. Nothing is lost or sent twice when you switch.
- **If Replyvoo can't answer**, for example if you run out of chats and credits, the chat goes back to Joinvoo's Team inbox by itself, so nobody is left waiting. It comes back to your Closer when it can answer again.
- **Photos, voice notes, videos, files, stickers and button taps** all pass both ways.
- **Manager accounts follow Telegram's rules.** Replies from a manager's own account can only go out within 24 hours of the customer's last message.

## What it costs

Each app has its own plan. Chats that come through Joinvoo are normal Replyvoo conversations: 30 are free every month on Spark, and Replyvoo's plans cover more. You can also pay for a Replyvoo plan or a credits pack from inside Joinvoo, with your Joinvoo credits (100 credits = $1) or by card, bank or crypto. If a purchase paid with Joinvoo credits is refunded, the refund goes back to your Joinvoo balance as credits.

## How to switch back

Pick **Joinvoo Team inbox** under Who answers chats in Joinvoo, or open **Settings → Connected apps** here and tap **Disconnect** (one bot or account, or all of them). Your chats stay in both apps.
